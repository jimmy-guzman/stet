## Switch themes

Press `t` to open the theme switcher: filter by name and move (or hover) to preview the whole UI live, `enter` to apply, `esc` to revert. By default stet uses `system`, a theme built from your terminal's own colors, and follows your terminal's appearance. The switcher also lists stet's own `stet-dark` and `stet-light`, the `opencode-*` and `cmux-*` pairs, the editor themes bundled with highlights (Catppuccin, Dracula, GitHub, Gruvbox, Nord, Tokyo Night, and more), and an all-grey `mono-dark` and `mono-light` pair it starts on when `NO_COLOR` is non-empty.

![The theme switcher listing themes with color swatches, previewing the highlighted one live](/screenshots/theme-switcher.png)

The switch lasts the session only; `ctrl-s` makes it permanent, writing the highlighted theme (with any other changed settings) to your config as targeted edits that keep your comments. To add your own, drop a Zed theme file into `~/.config/stet/themes/`; stet derives its whole UI from the theme's palette, as the [configuration reference](/docs/reference/configuration#themes) explains.
